Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The current Slow Linen website is designed to work without non-essential cookies or cross-site tracking.

Cookies on this website

We do not currently set analytics, personalization or advertising cookies. Product search, sorting and filtering run in your browser and do not require a tracking cookie.

Strictly necessary technology

The hosting or security provider may use essential technical mechanisms to deliver pages, prevent abuse and maintain security. These are not used by Slow Linen to build advertising profiles.

External websites

narton.shop is a separate website and may use its own cookies when you follow an outbound link. Its cookie banner, preferences and policy govern that activity. You can review and manage those settings on the merchant website.

Browser controls

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary technology may affect security or site delivery, but the core Slow Linen catalogue does not depend on optional cookies.

Changes

If analytics, embedded media or other optional technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before activation.

For purchases, order support and merchant policies, visit narton.shop.
