Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Slow Linen does not operate accounts, checkout or payment processing. This policy explains the limited information involved when you browse this static website.

Information you provide

The website does not include account registration, payment forms, newsletter collection or a direct contact form. Search and filter terms are processed in your browser to display products and are not transmitted to a Slow Linen database.

Technical information

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process standard technical logs such as IP address, requested page, browser type, device information and timestamps for security, reliability and diagnostics. Hosting arrangements determine retention.

Browser storage

The current website does not use local storage for user profiles or tracking. Filter selections can appear in the page URL so you can share or revisit a filtered view.

Outbound links

When you select a purchase or merchant link, you visit narton.shop. The merchant may receive standard referral information and handles information under its own privacy policy. Slow Linen does not receive your card details or merchant account password.

Analytics and advertising

The current website does not install third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If this changes, this policy and the cookie policy should be updated before those tools are enabled.

Your choices

You can browse without creating an account, clear browser data through your browser settings, and review the merchant’s privacy options before purchasing. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the organization processing the information.

Policy updates

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised update date.

External merchant

For purchases, order support and merchant policies, visit narton.shop.
