Can your little detective help Santa save Christmas?

Oh no! Santa's magical Christmas gift list has disappeared just before Christmas. Gather your pencils, call your little detectives, and follow the clues through a delightful holiday mystery.

Santa's Christmas Mystery Adventureis a colorful, printable detective puzzle game for children ages4–10. Complete 10 festive missions, collect secret clue letters and solve the final challenge to discover where Santa hid his missing list.

19 printable pages • 10 puzzles & games • Parent answer key included

10 Christmas detective missions

  • Christmas Mystery Hunt — find hidden objects in Santa's workshop
  • Santa's Secret Code — decode picture, number and letter-shift messages
  • Reindeer Maze Challenge — guide Ruby to Santa's sleigh
  • Christmas Word Scramble — solve festive word puzzles
  • Who Stole the Cookies? — follow clues to catch the cookie thief
  • Spot the Difference — find seven festive changes
  • Santa's Missing Gifts — match presents, finish patterns and count gifts
  • Christmas Memory Match — cut out and match eight pairs of cards
  • Elf Logic Puzzle — work through a North Pole logic challenge
  • Save Christmas! — unlock the final mystery using clues from the first nine missions

What's inside your download

  • One19-page printable PDFin US Letter size (8.5 × 11 inches)
  • 10 illustrated puzzle missions with different age suggestions
  • Detective ID, instructions and mission tracker
  • Official Christmas Detective Excellence certificate
  • Two-page parent answer key and tips for grown-ups
  • Bonus printable backs for the Memory Match cards

How to play

  1. Download the PDF and print the pages you want at 100% / Actual Size.
  2. Complete Missions 1–9 and record each clue letter on the tracker.
  3. Save Mission 10 for last, then put the clues together to solve Santa's mystery.
  4. Use the parent answer key if your detective needs a little help!

Great for Christmas family activities, holiday parties, homeschool and classroom fun. Younger children may enjoy playing with an adult's help. Print in color for the brightest experience; the pages also work in black and white.
