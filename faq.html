Learn how digital products, external checkout, delivery and our independent affiliate relationship work.

Are these physical planners?

No. The collection consists of digital downloads. No physical planner, journal or printed pages will be shipped.

Who processes my order?

Orders and payments are processed on narton.shop. Slow Linen is an independent affiliate storefront and does not operate checkout or access your payment details.

How will I receive my files?

Follow the digital-delivery instructions supplied by the merchant at checkout or in your order email. Slow Linen does not send files or maintain order accounts.

Can I print and annotate the PDFs?

Many products are designed for printing and can also be used in compatible PDF-annotation apps. Check the individual product description for exact file types, page sizes and features.

Can I share or resell downloaded files?

No. Unless a specific product states otherwise, downloads are for personal, non-commercial use. Redistribution, resale, sharing and commercial use are prohibited.

Where can I request order support or a refund?

Contact the merchant through narton.shop. The merchant’s order terms, delivery terms and refund policy apply to your purchase.
